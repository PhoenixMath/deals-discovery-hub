Overview

Deals Discovery Hub Automation is a personal content-management application that may integrate with Pinterest through the official Pinterest API.

Information Accessed

When the account owner authorizes Pinterest access using Pinterest OAuth 2.0, the application may access authorized Pinterest account information, Pins, and boards necessary to provide its content-management functionality.

Authentication

The application does not collect or store Pinterest passwords, login credentials, or browser session cookies. Authentication is performed through Pinterest OAuth 2.0.

Use of Information

Pinterest information is used only to create, organize, manage, or publish content requested by the authenticated user.

Data Sharing

Pinterest information is not sold or shared with third parties for unrelated advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Storage

OAuth tokens and limited application data may be securely stored when necessary to maintain the authorized Pinterest integration.

Revoking Access

Users may revoke Pinterest authorization at any time through their Pinterest account settings.
